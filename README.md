FROM ZERO TO TRACKING
YOUR CREW IN 6 STEPS
No jargon, no marketing fluff — just what actually happens when you use SpideyTracker, in the order you'll actually do it.

THE WALKTHROUGH
🕸️ CREATE OR JOIN A PARTY
Open the app and either start a new party (you get an invite code to share) or type in a code someone sent you. Up to 8 people per party.

You can be in more than one party at once — a switcher at the top lets you jump between them instantly. Nothing crosses over: different pins, different chat, different everything, per party.

📡 TURN ON LIVE TRACKING (OPTIONAL)
Tap "LIVE" on the map and your location starts sharing with your party in real time — shown as a distinct pulsing marker, never confused with a saved pin.

It's entirely opt-in per session. Turn it off any time and you disappear from the live map immediately; your past pins and journal entries are unaffected either way.

📸 DROP A PHOTO PIN
Tap anywhere on the map to drop a pin, write a caption, and attach up to 5 photos. Tap an existing pin and watch a pixel Spider-Man descend on a web to hand you a comic-panel photo frame.

Pins are shared with your whole party instantly — everyone sees the same map.

📖 REVISIT IT ALL IN THE JOURNAL
Every pin your party has ever dropped lives in one scrollable timeline, grouped by date, newest first — so you can actually find that one photo again without hunting across the map.

🔒 TALK IN ENCRYPTED CHAT
Each party has its own group chat, end-to-end encrypted. Messages live only on your own device once delivered — nothing sits around on a server waiting to be read.

🎭 MAKE IT YOURS
Pick your own live-location mask and your own memory-pin spider + color from the settings. Small pixel-font name tags keep everyone distinct even if two people pick the same look.
